# 📚 dewarr - Your Personal Audiobook Librarian

## 🚀 What Is dewarr?

dewarr is a free, self-hosted application that turns your computer into a personal audiobook hub. It helps you discover new audiobooks, keep track of what you want to read, and automatically download them—all without needing any technical knowledge.

Think of dewarr as your personal librarian that works around the clock. It connects to popular book-tracking services like Goodreads and Hardcover, so the books on your "want to read" list automatically become audiobooks ready for you to enjoy.

## ⭐ Key Features

- **Automatic Discovery**: dewarr finds audiobooks based on your reading lists and preferences
- **Goodreads Integration**: Syncs with your Goodreads account to pull books you've marked as "want to read"
- **Hardcover Integration**: Works with Hardcover, another popular book-tracking service
- **Audiobookshelf Integration**: Connects seamlessly to Audiobookshelf, a popular audiobook player and server
- **Automatic Downloads**: When a new audiobook becomes available, dewarr downloads it for you automatically
- **Self-Hosted**: You control everything—your data stays on your computer, not in the cloud
- **Docker Support**: Runs smoothly with Docker for advanced users who want easy setup
- **Simple Interface**: Clean, easy-to-navigate design that anyone can use

## 🔧 System Requirements

Before you begin, make sure your computer meets these basic requirements:

- **Operating System**: Windows 10 or Windows 11
- **Internet Connection**: Required for downloads and syncing
- **Storage Space**: At least 5 GB of free space for audiobooks
- **Memory**: 4 GB RAM or more recommended
- **Processor**: Any modern processor (Intel or AMD)

## 🔑 Setting Up Your Account

### Connect Your Book Services

1. **Click "Settings"** in the top right corner of the main screen
2. **Select "Connections"** from the menu
3. **Choose Goodreads or Hardcover** and click "Connect"
4. **Sign in** to your chosen service when prompted
5. **Allow access** to let dewarr read your book lists

### Connect Audiobookshelf

1. In the same "Connections" menu, find "Audiobookshelf"
2. Enter your Audiobookshelf server address and credentials
3. Click "Test Connection" to verify everything works
4. Click "Save" to finish

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is dewarr free?

Yes, dewarr is completely free to use. It's an open-source project, meaning anyone can use and improve it.

### Do I need a Goodreads or Hardcover account?

No, but having one makes dewarr much more useful. Without these accounts, you'll need to add books manually.

### Can I use dewarr without Audiobookshelf?

Absolutely. Audiobookshelf integration is optional. Without it, you can still download audiobooks and play them with any player.

### How often does dewarr check for new audiobooks?

By default, dewarr checks every 6 hours. You can change this in Settings, from every 30 minutes to once a day.

### What happens if my internet connection drops?

dewarr will pause downloads and resume them automatically when your connection returns. Your library and settings are saved locally.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### dewarr won't start

1. Make sure you have at least 4 GB of free memory
2. Check that your antivirus isn't blocking the application
3. Try running dewarr as administrator (right-click > Run as Administrator)

### Downloads fail

- Verify your internet connection is stable
- Check available storage space
- Ensure Audiobookshelf is running and accessible

### Can't connect to Goodreads/Hardcover

- Log out and log back in to the service
- Check that you've approved dewarr's access request
- Try reconnecting in Settings > Connections

## 📊 Performance Optimization
